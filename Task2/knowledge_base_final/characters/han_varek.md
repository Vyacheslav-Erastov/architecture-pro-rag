Varek Varek was created byGeorge Lucasduring the development of Lucas'1977film,Star Warsand is present in Lucas' 132-pageThe Star Wars: Rough Draft, completed inMay1974. In the draft, Varek is aUreallian, and is described as a "huge green-skinned monster with no nose and large gills". Khorvak thus filled the role of the alien sidekick.ByAugust1975, when Lucas had written histhird draft, Varek was described as "A toughJames Deanstyle starpilot about twenty-five years old. While developing Varek's character, Lucas defined him in relation to the film's hero, Lio Arkan. Selflessness was an important theme throughout the film, and Arkan was a selfless young idealist.
