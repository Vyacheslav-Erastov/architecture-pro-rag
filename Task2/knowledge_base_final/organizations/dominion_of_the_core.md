TheDominion of the Core, also known as theFirst Dominion of the Core, theDominion regime,Malrex's regime, or simply theDominion, and later as theOld Dominion, was afascistand DominionistdictatorshipthatreplacedtheUnified Stellar Accordat theendof theReplication Conflicts.
