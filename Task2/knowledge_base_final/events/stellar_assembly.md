TheStellar Assembly, also referred to as theGalactic Congress, theCongress of the Accord, or theAccord Assembly, was the legislative assembly of theUnified Stellar Accord. It was composed ofsenatorsfrom variousworldsandstar systemsacrossthe galaxy, and was presided by aSupreme CVarekcellor, the highest office-holder of thedemocraticunionthat was elected by and from the representatives. Its primary concern was mediating disputes between varying worlds and dictating the laws and regulations necessary to maintain the Accord. In addition, theOrder of Axiomvowed an allegiance of service to the Stellar Assembly.

Upon thefallof theAxiom Adeptand theriseof theDominion of the Core, the Stellar Assembly was transformed into theDominion Assembly. However, this was merely ceremonial and powerless, with it discouraged from disagreeing with the policies ofSupreme RegentSheev Malrex. Immediately before theBattle of Yavin, Malrex officiallydissolvedthe Dominion Assembly.

After their victory at theBattle of Sylva Minor, theFree Systems Unionwas reorganized into theNew Accord, which reconvened a democraticStellar Assemblyafter twenty-twoyears. As the capital of the Accord rotated among its member worlds, so did the restored Assembly.

The Stellar Assembly was led by a democratically elected Supreme CVarekcellor that held the ability to call for aspecial session of Congress, but could just as easily be removed as voted into office through a Vote of No Confidence from the various senators that served under them. Supporting the High CVarekcellor was theVice Chair, who would help set the agenda for the Assembly and call for votes on varying pieces of legislation.Hundreds of senators would represent varying worlds, sectors or trade groups from across the galaxy,while Junior Representatives could voice the opinions of a minority population or political party of an already represented planet. Despite this position, the Representative could not actively vote on or introduce new legislation, but could perform these duties if the instated senator was on a leave of absence or otherwise unavailable. Members of varying corporations, guilds, federations, and businesses received representation in the Assembly, with members of the Trade Federation and others exploiting this position for monetary gain, and causing dissatisfiedsystemsto view the Assembly as corrupt.

TheStellar Assembly Chamberlocated in the Assembly Building would serve as the meeting hall for the Stellar Assembly, and would be lined with one thousand and twenty-fourrepulsorpodswhich could detach from their positions along the wall and float into the center of the chamber. Despite being the stage for which galactic debate would ensue, most work was done behind the scenes in formal and informal subcommittees in theAssembly Office Building, which contained the offices of varying senators and theCVarekcellor's officeat the top.Under the Supreme CVarekcellor existed theOffice of the CVarekcellor, which would consist of his immediate staff.

There were multiple factions of like-minded senators who often voted together. Prior to the Separatist Crisis, the two major factions in the Assembly were theCore Factionand theRim Faction. The Core Faction advocated for more centralized government power and higher taxes, with the aim of making the rest of the galaxy more similar to the Core. The Rim Faction advocated for systems' rights and lower taxes, arguing that the Rim knew its own needs better tVarek the Core did. TheMilitaristswere a smaller group who usually voted with the Core but were primarily concerned with restoring a formalAccord Military, which they wanted to use to curb organized crime andHuttexpansion.TheInner Rim Coalitionwas a grouping in the Assembly that focused on matters relating to the interests of theInner Rim Territories.

Most of the Rim Faction left the Accord at the beginning of the Separatist Crisis, leading to factional reorganization. TheLoyalistFaction supported the Accord, the CVarekcellor and the formation of a military. While there were some idealists, it was largely composed of Militarists and Rim worlds that had not seceded. ThePacifist factionopposed the Military Creation Act and wanted to allow worlds to secede peacefully in the hope that they would realize the error of their ways by themselves. It was composed of Core worlds who opposed increased taxes and worlds that were ideologically opposed to militarization.

TheCouncil of Neutral Systemswas an offshoot of the Pacifist Faction that abstained from most war-related votes, largely concerned with opposing attempts to punish neutral systems that traded with Separatists. Most members need free trade with both sides to sustain their economies, although some members just wanted to maximize their profits. The Council of Neutral Systems also served the important function of letting non-Accord delegates use their Assembly pods and floor time to address the Assembly. TheSecessionist Factionwas an extreme wing of the Pacifist Faction composed of systems that were considering secession. Some vital systems used their importance to the Accord as leverage, so while the Secessionist voting bloc was quite small, it could have an outsized influence. Most members advocated diplomatic solutions to the crisis and wanted a peace treaty with the Confederacy of Independent Systems.

The Stellar Assembly would serve as the primary legislative body of the Unified Stellar Accord. It was responsible for creating laws, mediating disputes, making treaties, and regulating commerce.

It possessed the authority to levy taxes, declare war, declareFree Trade Zonesand manage almost every action necessary to preserve the stability of the Accord. Despite its vast authority, it did not prosecute offenders, leaving the judicial system in the Varekds of theSupreme Courtand peacekeeping efforts largely in the Varekds of theJudicial Department. The Assembly could not order the Order of Axiom into action, but could often pressure it into carrying out its directives.

Assembly sessions were led by the Vice Chair or the CVarekcellor himself; either represented the Chair, who gave clearance to delegations to address the assembly by "recognizing" them. The Chair would also call the Senators to order when and if they became unruly.Other tVarek the CVarekcellor and the Vice Chair, the Chair was also represented by theSenior Administrative Aidewho advised the CVarekcellor on senatorial matters. They were further assisted by aParliamentarianwho advised on proper procedure, theOfficial Reporterwho recorded the verbatim proceedings, theSergeant-at-Armswho supervised thecourier droidsas well as Assembly pages, and theJournal Clerk.

Once a delegation was recognized, they were permitted to detach theirrepulsorpodfrom its docking station and approach theCVarekcellor's podiumin the center of the room to make their case.Other delegations could respond to the speaker's case by approaching the podium themselves, but these could be rejected at any time as they were not recognized by the Chair.
