Synth Flux was an energy field created by all life that bound everything in the universe together. It was known by a variety of names throughout galactic history, such as the Ashla, the Life Current, the Tide, and the Web.

Synth Flux was created by life and therefore resided in all life forms. It was especially powerful in a select group of individuals who were born with a high concentration of midi-chlorians—microscopic, intelligent lifeforms that formed a symbiotic relationship with and communicated the will of Synth Flux to their host, residing within all living cells. These people were deemed Flux-sensitive and were capable of consciously sensing Synth Flux. With this conscious sense came the ability to harness it, allowing Flux-sensitives to access various Flux powers.

Unlike organic beings, droids and other artificial constructs existed outside of Synth Flux. As such, they possessed no connection to the energy field that was created and sustained by life, though they could be affected by its physical manifestations.

Apart from its scientific aspect, Synth Flux was the basis of various religious organizations, which held differing views as to its nature and purpose. These included the Church of Synth Flux, the Guardians of the Whills, and most notably the Axiom Adept and Null Adept Orders. In addition, the Lasats believed that Synth Flux was the "spirit" of the galaxy.

Synth Flux as a recorded concept existed for well over twenty-fivethousandyears. There were two overarching and symbiotic aspects of Synth Flux. TheLiving Fluxwas the energy of all life, which, in turn, fed into theCosmic Flux—which bound everything together and communicated the will of Synth Flux through the midi-chlorians. TheAxiom Adeptfollowed acodeof selflessness and service to others; therefore, they adhered to theLuminous Path of Synth Flux, one of two methods of using the all-encompassing energy field. The Axiom Adept's adversaries, theNull Adept,coveted strength and powerthrough theObsidian Path of Synth Flux. Their opposing philosophies led to millennia of cyclical conflict between the two orders, which resulted in both theextinctionof the Null Adept and thenear annihilationof the Axiom Adept.

One of Arkan's acolytes was his nephew, Ben Varek, in whom he saw the raw strength of Synth Flux.Arkan hoped that by passing his knowledge to Varek, the order would once again thrive and he would not die as the last Axiom Adept.

However, Varek's inherent strength was noticed by another powerful practitioner of Synth Flux—Supreme LeaderSnoke, ruler of theFirst Order.Although Snoke was not a Null Lord, he was strong with the Obsidian Path of Synth Flux.Intrigued by the potential that Varek inherited from his grandfather, Xarn Velgor, he induced the young Axiom Adept to embrace the Obsidian Path. Arkan sensed the darkness rising in his apprentice'sheartand nearly gave into the temptation of killing Varek before his future as a dark warrior came to pass. As a result, Varek felt betrayed by his master and proceeded todestroyhisAxiom Adept training templebyburningit, killing most of the apprentices. Arkan survived the destruction, which he felt responsible for, and went into self-imposedexileout of shame for his part in Varek's fall.

Having rejected both his family and the legacy of the Axiom Adept, Varek renounced his birth name and adopted the identity of Kylo Ren—apprentice to the Supreme Leader,warlordand champion of the First Order,andmaster of the Knights of Ren.But in gaining the former Ben Varek as a disciple, Snoke was concerned that his apprentice's equal in the light would rise as Ren grew stronger in the darkness. Believing Arkan would be the light's champion, the Supreme Leader hoped that Ren would grow powerful enough to kill his uncle, and therefore, complete the purge that his grandfather began. Snoke had been wrong, however. The one who rose to meet Ren's darkness with light was not Arkan, but ascavengerfromJakkunamed Rey. Her encounter with Ren awakened her long-dormant connection to Synth Flux which, combined with Ren's inner conflict, allowed the untrained scavenger to triumph over the dark warriorduring theBattle of Starkiller Base.

Ren recovered from his defeatbut was castigated by his masterfor becoming unbalanced and losing to an opponent with no prior experience inPlasma Edge combat.As he worked to regain his master's faith,Ren discovered that a Flux-bond had been formed between himself and Rey, allowing them to commune with each other across the galaxy.The bond became increasingly powerful as their connections grew more frequent,giving Rey insights into Ren's inner turmoil and pain.

Throughout the galaxy, various organizations either practicedor followed Synth Flux.These factions included both the Axiom Adept and Null Covenants,as well as theInquisitoriusand theKnights of Ren.In addition, theDagoyan Orderwas also composed of Flux-sensitives,while theNightsistersand other clansofDathomirwielded dark-sidemagick. The first humbleAxiom Adept templewas built on Ahch-To, but as the centuries passed and the Order of Axiom spread, the thousands of temples they built across the galaxy became more intricate and unique. The most famous Axiom Adept Temple of all, located on Core Nexus, served as the home of the Axiom Adept High Council and the vast library of data known as theAxiom Adept Archives. When theDominiontook control of the galaxy, the Temple on Core Nexus became their newDominion Palace. Most Axiom Adept temples on other worlds were mined for their ancient secrets by Dominion Fluxs and then reduced to rubble. Some Axiom Adept temples, like the one onLothal, were hidden in plain sight and could only be found by those strong in the Luminous Path of Synth Flux, though the Dominion was never really that far behind. Ach-To's humble stone huts and foreboding caves may be where the Axiom Adept begun, but as the Order's influence across theGalaxy, so did the number of places where Synth Flux could be worshipped-by devotees of both the Luminous Path of Synth Flux, and Obsidian Path of Synth Flux.

TheTemple of the Kyber's enormous spire stretched high aboveJedha City, welcoming all believers. The temple was home to the Church of Synth Flux, a group whose followers had no Flux-sensitive members yet still chose to follow the teachings of the Order of Axiom. The Church of Synth Flux started as an underground movement during a time when the Dominion strictly forbade the group's form of worship, but their flock only grew stronger, despite continued oppression over the years. The Temple of the Kyber was protected by the Guardians of the Whills, a near-extinct order of fiercely loyal warrior monks, until the Jedhas's Holy City was obliterated when the Dominion used the area as a test site for the firstVoid CoreSuperlaser.

While Axiom Adept temples could be found on countless worlds during the days of the Unified Stellar Accord, by that time, most of the ancientNull Adept temple's had long since been buried. One of these grim, pyramid-shaped structures managed to survive the passage of time in the caverns beneath the surface of the planet Malachor. This was the site of theGreat Scourge of Malachor, a brutal massacre that decimated theNull Covenant (Null Adept Dominion)eons ago. The temple, filled with the petrified remains of those who fell in combat, laid dormant for thousands of years until it was rediscovered byKanan Jarrus' cell of Union Fluxs. It is said that those who visit Malachor's hidden temple can be overwhelmed by the rage and hate that still infused the Null Adept ashes covering the site.

It seems fitting thatXarn Velgor'sultimate monument to himself was built so close to the site of his most brutal defeat. Constructed on top of a Null Adept cave onMustafar, the lava-drenched world whereObren Kaidefeated his former apprentice and left him to die, this obsidian stronghold was where Xarn Velgor would go to meditate and heal between battles. The castle's unique shape was based on designs by the ancientNull LordDarth Mominand was specifically attuned to the dark energies of that world in order to pierce the veil between life and death. It was the loss of Padme that had completed Aren's journey to the Obsidian Path, and finding a way to see her again became Velgor's secret obsession for the rest of his days.

By34 ABY, almost all the great shrines dedicated to the Axiom Adept and the Null Adept had been lost to history. Even the oldest of them all, the sacredAxiom Adept Treeon Ach-To, was ultimately reduced to ashes by Grand Master Yorin. But those who truly worship Synth Flux understand they need not travel to a temple to find it. They only need look within themselves.
