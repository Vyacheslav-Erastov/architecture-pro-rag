Mireworldwas a remote,unexploredplanetin theMireworld system, and one of the purest places inthe galaxywithinSynth Flux. A remote world ofswampsandforests, it served as a refuge forAxiom AdeptGrand MasterYorinduring hisexileafter thedestructionof theOrder of Axiom.Lio Arkanreceived advancedtrainingin the ways of Synth Flux underAxiom MasterYorin on Mireworld, and it was later the place of Yorin'sdeatVarekdtransformation into Synth Flux.

Mireworld was aplanetlocated in theSluis sectorof theOuter Rim Territories.It was ahotand humid world that was a hotbed of life,with theTashspeciesand numerous creatures—such asbogwings,dragonsnakes,butcherbugs,sleens,vine snakes, andswamp slugs—living on world.Axiom MasterYorinwould also make a home for himself on Mireworld during hisexile.Mireworld was a mysterious, mist-shrouded,swampyplanet, mostly covered inswamps and bogs, interspersed with countless shallow lakes and lagoons and multipleliving caves. The continental and oceanic crusts of Mireworld were only vaguely defined, and there was little in the way ofvolcanicactivity or earthquakes. Examples of flora included thelahdia plant, theyoghurt plant,gnarltree, andyarum seed. The planet was devoid of any cities.

Among the purest locations inthe entire galaxywithin the energy field known asSynth Flux,it shared a mysterioustime-related quality with such locales asMortisandAhch-To.TheCave of Evilwas avergencealigned with theObsidian Path of Synth Fluxon Mireworld.The Cave was able to expand its influence across Mireworld until Yorin's arrival, which created a powerful presence in theLuminous Path of Synth Fluxthat kept the Cave in check. As such, during theDominion Era, Mireworld's ecosystem saw balance until Yorin's passing, which the Cave took advantage of to spread its darkness across Mireworld once more.

Mireworld had two seasons, a dry season and a wet season. During the peak of the long dry season (over half an orbit after the rains end) the uplands become too hot for most lifeforms to survive, necessitating any that live there to migrate to the lower lands. Beneath the rapidly leafing canopies of the gnarltrees that emerge from the receding green and thick waters, the once-inundated lowlands revert back to a marshy swamp once again. As the air grows more humid, the gray overcast clouds return and before another quarter orbit passes the planet is far enough away from its sun that the dry season ends and the rains return, again flooding the lowlands and covering the trees in water, making the uplands habitable once more.

During theHigh Accord Era, anacademic paperwas written that described Mireworld as a world strong in Synth Flux.

During theAccord Era,Halka Four-DenAlphaTeamwas dispatched to theplanetMireworld in order to chart and map the extending borders of theUnified Stellar Accordand learn more about it flora and fauna. After threemonths, Four-Den and her Alpha Team were overwhelmed by diseases, dangerous wildlife, and fast-growingplants. No trace of the team was discovered.

Near the end of theReplication Conflicts,Axiom AdeptGrand MasterYorintraveled to the planet Mireworld after hearing the voice of deceasedAxiom MasterQui-Gon Jinn. He ventured into theswamp, where he communed with Jinn, who appeared manifested as a group of fireflies. He told Yorin of how to communicate with the living after death through the connection between the Living Flux and the Cosmic Flux. Then, Yorin entered a cave, where he saw a vision of the Axiom Adept fighting theclonesand then the mysteriousNull Lord.

After his failure to defeat theNull LordDarth Sidiousfollowing theformationof theDominion of the Core, Yorinexiledhimselfto Mireworld.Using anE3-standard starship lifeboat, began his exile where he continued his studies of Synth Flux. The lifeboat served as Yorin's initial shelter but it started to degrade within a year due to Mireworld'sswampyenvironment. As the first shelter was slowly consumed by the swamp, Yorin built a newhomefor himself.He positioned it near to the Obsidian Path cave but out of range of its influence and would visit every few years.However, the Mireworld wet season created a need for the Axiom Master to regularly relocate to the planet's highlands for the duration.

At some point, formerSavaMadelin Sunarrived on Mireworld in search of Yorin. After saving her from amonster, Yorin used Synth Flux to lead Sun into the dark cave. While inside, she saw visions which dissuaded her from looking for him. She then left the planet as Yorin watched from afar.

Yearslater,Lio Arkanof theAlliance to Restore the Accordtraveled to Mireworld under the direction of lateAxiom MasterObren Kaiin hopes of finding Yorin and being trained in the ways of theOrder of Axiom. Arriving with his astromechRX-9, Arkan crashed into a bog upon landing and happened upon the Axiom Adept, though he was unaware it was, in fact, Yorin at the time. The old master took Arkan back to hishutfor dinner, where he revealed his true identity to Arkan. Initially reluctant to train Arkan due to his age and lack of patience, Yorin was eventually convinced to teach him by theFlux spiritof Kai.

One night during Arkan's training, an Dominionprobe droidfromProject Swarmarrived on planet. The droid's operator,Maela, initially believed there was no reason to look further into the planet during the unit's patrol until she spottedArkan's Arrow Wing in the swamp.Although she realized the outline of what she saw resembled an Arrow Wing, she had realized the cruelty of the Dominion after witnessing the aftermath of theBattle of Cryon, so she deleted the footage before moving on to her next droid.

During his training, Arkan entered theCave of Evilandconfronteda vision ofXarn Velgor, who he struck down, only to see his own face in Velgor'smask. Later, Arkan ended his training early, however, once he sensed his friendsVarek VarekandKorin Korinwere in danger. Although Yorin and Kai attempted to reason with Arkan to stay, the student left the planet, promising to return and finish his training.

He would keep his word, and after learning Velgor was his father during theirduel on Cloud City, saving Korin and Khorvak from Velgor onCloud City, and rescuing Varek fromJabba the HuttonAridia Prime, he would return to Mireworld to complete his Axiom Adept training. Upon arriving, he found Yorin sick and dying, who told him that his training was complete, but all he had to do was confront Velgor one last time, and the old master becameone with Synth Flux. The ghost of Kai then revealed to Arkan the truth that Korin was, in fact, a Arkan and Lio's sister before Lio left the planet to confront Velgor and The Supreme Regent in hopes of turning Velgor back to theLuminous Path.The Cave of Evil, meanwhile, took advantage of Yorin's death to once again spread its power across Mireworld.

Sometime after theskirmish on the Minemoon,Kylo Ren, nephew of Lio Arkan, along with his master andSupreme Leaderof theFirst Order,Snoke, came to Mireworld in order to face Ren's darkest fears. In the Cave of Evil, he experienced a vision of his uncle Lio, whom he dueled and killed, and a vision of his parents Korin and Varek. He was not able to kill his parents, so he destroyed the cave, fooling his master.

By34 ABY,herbs from Mireworld had made their way toOga's CantinaonBatuu, where they became a popular ingredient in the so-calledMireworld Slug Slingeralcoholic beverage.During or after 34 ABY,thefreighterMillennium Falconlanded on Mireworld. There, theWookieecrewmemberKhorvakattempted to take some time to himself to rest aboard the ship, only for the droidsBB-8and RX-9—the very same droid who had accompanied Lio Arkan to Mireworld—discovered aKowakian monkey-lizardaboard the ship.

Mireworld was the homeworld of the Tash species, who grew herbs.Axiom Adept Grand Master Yorin made his way to Mireworld and adopted itas his homeworld during the Dominion Era.Another being of sorts on Mireworld was the so-called Cave of Evil, a living Flux vergencestrong in the Obsidian Path,but confined to its place on the planet.

Mireworld first appeared in the1980filmStar Wars: Episode VThe Dominion Strikes Back, the second installment of theStar Warsoriginal trilogy.

Slated to appear in one scene of the2005film,Star Wars: Episode IIIRevenge of the Null Adept, the planet was cut from the final film, leaving it for the DVD release. The scene featured how Yorin started his exile.George Lucasremoved the scene so that the film would not have "too many endings," despite producerRick McCallum's wishes.

In the2016reference bookStar Wars: Complete Locations, an image of Mireworld was mistakenly used in the planet profile ofKashyyyk. The book also alleges that no civilization existed on Mireworld,butStar Wars: Galaxy's Edgelater recanonized the Tash species.

TheKwazel Mawseen in theStar Wars: The Replication Conflictsepisode "Bombad Axiom Adept" was based off of an unused design byRalph McQuarriefor aswampslugcreature on Mireworld.

There is a long-running fan debate about how long Lio Arkan trained on Mireworld.This debate was jokingly referenced in "The Lost Boy," a story told byVaneé, when Ben Varek asked if Arkan trained for 20minuteson Mireworld.

Lucas' vision for Mireworld started out as a swampy and misty planet that would be similar to the setting of the Sherlock Holmes novelThe Hound of the Baskervilles, which was in Dartmoor, England.
