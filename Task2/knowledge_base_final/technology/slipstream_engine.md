# Slipstream Engine

TheSlipstream Engine,also known aswarp drive,was apropulsion systemthat allowed astarshipto reachlightspeedand traverse the void between stars in the alternate dimension ofSlipstream Space. As a consequence, the Slipstream Engine was a key instrument in shapinggalacticsociety, trade,politics, and war.

The Slipstream Engine required a functionalSlipstream Engine motivatorto operate. The vessel then traveled along a programmed course until it dropped back into normal space—realspace—at its destination.

A Slipstream Engine only functioned to keep a vessel in Slipstream Space, and should a Slipstream Engine be forcibly deactivated or destroyed during transit, the ship was violently and instantly pulled back into realspace.

Being short-range craft, moststarfighterslacked a Slipstream Engine. This was notably the case with theDominion of the Core'sRift Interceptors, which had to be ferried to combat zones aboard larger ships. However, theArrow WingandA-wingstarfighters used by theFree Systems Unionwere fitted with Slipstream Engines, allowing them to make long-range jumps.

Upon entering Slipstream Space, a ship emittedcronau radiation, which was possible to detect with specializedsensorsuites.

Large objects in normal space cast "mass shadows" in Slipstream Space, thus Slipstream Space jumps required accurate plotting to avoid collisions, which were often fatal.

Later technologies could pull vessels out of Slipstream Space.
